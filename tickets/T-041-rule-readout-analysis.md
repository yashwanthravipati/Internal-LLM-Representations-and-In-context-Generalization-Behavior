# T-041 — Rule-readout analysis: does the inferred rule appear in the J-lens? (H4)

## Context
The J-lens paper found that implicit properties (part of speech, tense) enter the J-space only **when the question asks for them**. We test whether **demonstrations alone** recruit the cued attribute ("mammal"/"bird" vs "wild"/"pet") into the verbalizable workspace, and whether that predicts correct generalization (H4).

## Tasks
- [ ] For each Exp 2 prompt, layer and position (`query_item`, `decision`): lens score and rank of the cued-attribute words vs the uncued-attribute words. Use the attribute words plus single-token synonyms (T-010/T-012).
- [ ] Rule-readout score = mean z-scored lens score(cued words) − mean(uncued words).
- [ ] **Positive control:** explicit-question prompts from T-013 (the property words should appear).
- [ ] **Negative control:** random-label-context prompts (no rule, so no systematic difference).
- [ ] Does the rule-readout score predict correctness on incongruent queries? Use the T-033 framework (item-held-out AUROC), with layer selection on validation.
- [ ] Layer profile: where does the rule appear (expect the intermediate band)?
- [ ] Qualitative gallery of slice-style readouts for the report.
- [ ] Write `results/exp3/RULE_READOUT.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The primary H4 metric (PLAN §6.9 item 3) is reported on the test split with a CI; both controls are reported.
- [ ] Figures and gallery generated by a script.

## Outputs
- `src/icl_geometry/analysis/exp3_readout.py`, `results/exp3/RULE_READOUT.md`, `figures/exp3_readout_*`.

## Notes & pitfalls
- Attribute words may be weak lens concepts in small models (the paper notes vocabulary effects, e.g. number words). Check that the positive control works before interpreting nulls.
- The numbers domain (even/odd) may behave like the paper's weak number-word category. Report it separately.

## References
